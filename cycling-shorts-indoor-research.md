## Indoor-Specific Tips

- **Thinner chamois is better indoors** — you don't need vibration dampening on a static trainer, and thinner pads breathe better and accumulate less sweat
- **Mesh and perforation matter more than compression** — heat management is the #1 issue on the trainer
- **Don't overthink it** — any quality bib short works indoors; indoor-specific ones just manage sweat better
- **Consider two pairs** — a premium pair for long sessions and a cheap pair (Triban) for quick 30-min efforts where comfort matters less
