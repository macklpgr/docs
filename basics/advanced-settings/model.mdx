Byword offers two powerful AI text generation models that you can select based on your content needs. You can toggle between these models in your [account settings](https://byword.ai/settings).

## Available models

### OpenAI - GPT4.5 (recommended)

Our most versatile model that has powered Byword since launch:
* Familiar GPT technology with advanced reasoning capabilities
* Leverages OpenAI's latest o3 reasoning model for article generation
* Well-tested with Byword's systems
* Comprehensive SEO optimization capabilities
* Best performance with Live Search features

### Anthropic - Claude Sonnet 3.7

Our newly integrated model that offers several advantages:
* Higher quality writing that sounds more natural and human
* Better at maintaining consistent tone throughout articles
* Improved contextual understanding of topics
* More nuanced handling of complex subjects
* Recently added to the platform

## Live Search & External Links

### Live Search
Byword now offers a Live Search feature that enhances your content with real-time information:
* Automatically searches for current data, statistics, and recent developments related to your topic
* Incorporates up-to-date information seamlessly into your articles
* Ideal for topics that require recent information or frequently changing data
* Works with both OpenAI and Claude models, though **OpenAI is recommended** for optimal performance

### External Links
When Live Search is enabled, you can also activate External Links:
* Automatically includes relevant external links to authoritative sources
* Links are added contextually on important phrases, statistics, or company names
* All external links include `rel="nofollow"` for SEO best practices
* Typically includes 4-6 high-quality links per article
* **Only available when Live Search is enabled**

<Note>
External Links require Live Search to be active, as the links are sourced from the live research data.
</Note>

## Choosing the right model

Your model selection affects all content generated through your account. Consider these factors when choosing:

* **Performance**: OpenAI provides the most comprehensive feature set, including enhanced reasoning for complex topics
* **Content style**: If you prioritize a specific writing style, Claude Sonnet offers excellent natural language flow
* **Live Search needs**: OpenAI provides superior integration with Live Search capabilities
* **API usage**: For Unlimited plan users, your selection affects your API consumption

<Info>
You can switch between models at any time to test which works best for your specific content needs.
</Info>
